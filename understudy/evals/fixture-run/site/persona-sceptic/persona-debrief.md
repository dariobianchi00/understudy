# Debrief — sceptic

**Q1 — In one sentence, what does this company do?**
A notes app that is supposed to remember things — I'm guessing from the name and from what my friend said, not from the page.

**Q2 — Who is it for? Is that you?**
The page doesn't say. Possibly me.

**Q3 — What would you do next, if anything? Why that?**
Leave. The cookie bar and the privacy page disagree, and the privacy page won't say who the partners are.

**Q4 — What did you want to know that the site never told you?**
Who they share data with. Whether there are tracking cookies. What "remembers everything" looks like.

**Q5 — Would you trust them with your money or your data? What made the difference?**
The About page made them real — a founder with a name, an address, a company number, and my data in Frankfurt. The privacy page took it back.

**Q6 — Does the site match the promise that brought you here?**
"It remembers everything." No — nothing on the site shows it remembering anything; the button that might have doesn't work.
