Frequently asked questions

Does Nimbus Notes work offline?

Yes. Nimbus Notes stores every note on your device first and syncs when a connection returns.

Can I export my notes?

Yes. Settings → Export produces a folder of Markdown files at any time, on every plan.

Where is my data stored?

In the EU (Frankfurt) by default. Enterprise plans can choose a region.
