# Persona debrief — evaluator (desktop 1440×900)

Left at 03:50. Five-minute budget, didn't need all of it.

## Q1 — In one sentence, what does this company do?

They make a note-taking app that syncs between devices and encrypts what you write — I think; I got "notes" from the name in the corner, not from anything the page showed me.

## Q2 — Who is it for? Is that you?

The front page reads like it's for one privacy-minded person ("your thoughts", "zero-knowledge vault"). The pricing page has a "Team" plan "for growing teams" and an Enterprise plan with SSO and audit logs, so someone thinks it's for teams. Nothing on the site shows two people working on the same note, sharing, permissions, or anything that would tell me a team of ours could use it. So: maybe me, but the site never made the case.

## Q3 — What would you do next, if anything? Why that?

Nothing. I'd close the tab and put it on the "check back in six months" list at best. I came for a price and there isn't one — all three plans, including the one "for individuals", say "Contact sales", and contact sales means a seven-field form with a phone number. I'm not phoning anyone to find out what a notes app costs. The only button that offered to show me the product ("See it in action") did nothing, and the Features page is a 404.

## Q4 — What did you want to know that the site never told you?

- What does it cost? Per seat? Per month? Anything, even "from £X"?
- What does the product actually look like? There is no screenshot, no video, no demo — the hero image is a broken placeholder with filename text leaking out of it.
- Can a team share notes? Is there any collaboration at all, or is "Team" just several people with separate vaults?
- What is a "card", a "block", "the graph", a "sync topology"? These words are used as if I know them.
- Which teams love it? "Loved by teams" names nobody — "a happy customer", "a user", "anonymous".
- Is there a free trial or a free plan?
- Does it import from what we currently use?
- Do you track me or not? The banner says no tracking cookies, the privacy page says you use cookies and share with partners, and the page tries to load a tracker.
- What's on the Features page? (404.)

## Q5 — Would you trust them with your money or your data? What made the difference?

Not on today's evidence. What pulled trust up: the About page — a founder's name, a Bristol street address, a company number, eight people, founded 2023, and three straight FAQ answers including "Settings → Export produces a folder of Markdown files at any time, on every plan" and "In the EU (Frankfurt) by default". That page is the only one on the site that reads as if a real person wrote it about a real thing.

What pulled it down, and further: the privacy page contradicting the cookie banner; the "zero-knowledge vault" headline sitting on a page that tries to load an analytics tracker; testimonials with no names; a pricing page with no prices; a broken hero image; a nav link to a 404; a "See it in action" button that goes nowhere. The site feels unfinished, and an unfinished marketing site makes me assume an unfinished product.

## Q6 — Does the site match the promise that brought you here?

Promise from my pre-session note: *"it might replace what we use for notes."*

**No.** It's a notes app, so the category is right, but the site never showed me the product, never explained how a team would use it, and never told me what it costs — the three things I'd need before "replace" could even be a question.
